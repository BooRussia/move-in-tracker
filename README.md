# Move-in list · Dunnellon

A one-page tracker for the move: house transfer tasks, the smart home plan, the shopping list, a price log, and a live countdown to Black Friday (Nov 27, 2026).

Live site: https://boorussia.github.io/move-in-tracker/

## What it does

- Check items off across House, Smart home, Shopping and Black Friday lists.
- Open any item for links: product pages, Google Shopping, Amazon, Walmart, Target and price history.
- Log prices you see (with store and date). Items with a target price show "At target" when a logged price hits it.
- Enter what you paid to track spending and savings against list price.
- Add your own shopping items, notes, and a move-in date.
- Export and import progress as a JSON file.

## Where progress is saved

In your browser's local storage. It does not sync between devices on its own. Use Export on one device and Import on the other.

## Files

- `index.html` — the whole site, no build step.
- `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md` — the brief and the locked visual language.
