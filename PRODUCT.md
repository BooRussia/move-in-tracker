# Product

## Audience
One household moving into a house in Dunnellon, FL (ZIP 34433, Citrus County) in early November 2026. They use it on a phone while shopping and on a laptop while planning.

## Purpose
Track the move-in: house transfer tasks, the smart home plan, the household shopping list, prices seen while shopping, and what to hold for Black Friday (Nov 27) and Cyber Monday (Nov 30).

## Constraints
- Static site on GitHub Pages. No backend, no accounts.
- Progress (checks, logged prices, paid amounts, notes) lives in the visitor's browser. Export and import move it between devices.
- Prices can't be fetched live from retailers from a static page. Each item links out to live prices and price history instead.
- Must work at phone width with no horizontal page scroll.
- The repo is public. No addresses, account numbers or names beyond what the original list showed.

## Voice
- Plain, short, second person.
- States facts and dates. No hype, no exclamation marks.
- Avoids "simply", "just", "easily".

## Evidence
- Source: the user's move-in list (Oct 31, 2026), with a house transfer checklist, smart home picks rated for Home Assistant, and a shopping list split into buy first, most expensive, and wait for Black Friday.
- The user asked for: check items off, click links, track prices, a live countdown to Black Friday, and "everything" else on the list.
- The source colour-coded "buy first" as blue. That maps to the accent here.
